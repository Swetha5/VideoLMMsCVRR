In this critical stage, competitors have one week to submit a detailed technical report. This report should encompass their methodological and data specifics. While including ablation studies is recommended to showcase the effectiveness of different components, it is not mandatory. This phase offers participants the chance to demonstrate the depth and robustness of their methods. The thoroughness of submissions is vital as they provide key insights into the strategies behind their results.

Second is the Test (Test) Phase, where the contestants will be submitting their approaches and methods in the hold-out dataset of CVRR-ES dataset. The test set consists of X open-ended question-answer (QA) pairs spanning X unique videos. The average video duration is X seconds, with maximum and minimum durations of X and Y seconds, respectively.

Test annotations are available here: : validation_data.zip

A public leaderboard will be available to track participants' performance.
Participants can choose whether to make a submission public or private, private submissions will not appear on the leaderboard and are not eligible for the final competition.
In order to be eligible for the final competition a submission must be public, and the email field must be filled in with a valid email address.
